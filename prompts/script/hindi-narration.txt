Hindi Narration Guidelines for Kids Shorts:
- Keep sentences under 8 words each.
- Use rhythmic, playful cadence (अरे वाह!, देखो तो सही!, कितना मज़ा आया!).
- Emphasize emotional expressions (खुशी, आश्चर्य, प्यार, मदद).
- Script must match exact visual timing (approx 2 words per second).
- Standard Hindi pronunciation suitable for TTS or human voiceover.
